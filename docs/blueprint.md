# **App Name**: Little Tummies, Big Smiles

## Core Features:

- Homepage with Toolbar and Summaries: Display site name on the left and a menu bar on the right with links to About Us, Recipes, Final Tips and Leave your comment sections. Present an educational and intuitive message in the hero section with summaries for each section with specific colors.
- About Us Section: Show the development group information including name (Neva) and slogan ('Un futuro educativo conectado'). Include team member information and a photo carousel with name, picture, and kid-friendly descriptions for each.
- Recipes Section: Display recipes that the user provided for food plans with Breakfast, Lunch, Snack/Dinner, including recommendation examples from Ana, Luis, Sofia and Diego.
- Final Tips Section: Show three nutritional tips as well a conclusion provided by the user to make healthier menus.
- Leave Your Comment Form: Include a text saying, 'If you leave your plate here, we'll send it to be made right away,' and then make a form input for leaving a comment.
- Footer: Link social media icons, a copyright notice, and the developer's name.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft Celadon Green (#ACE1AF), representing health and growth in a subtle way.
- Background color: Light Cyan (#E0FFFF), to maintain a cheerful but clean and airy atmosphere.
- Accent color: Pale Goldenrod (#EEE8AA) which is analogous and creates enough separation to bring important options to users' attention.
- Font pairing: 'Poppins' (sans-serif) for headlines for a precise and fashionable look, and 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body text due to its warmth and readability for extended text.
- Use clear, simple icons that resonate with a younger audience; mostly pictograms and outlined styles.
- All views must be responsive, with a toolbar on top. Summaries on the home page should be visually separated with distinct background colors or cards. Photo carousels on 'About Us'.
